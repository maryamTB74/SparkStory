A seed needs water, warmth and light to grow, and it grows roots downward before it
grows a shoot upward.

---

Plants cannot walk, so they spread by sending seeds away on the wind, on water, or
carried by animals.

---

A tree grows a new ring inside its trunk each year, so the rings can be counted to
tell its age.

---

Leaves fall from some trees before winter because there is not enough light to keep
them working.

---

Sunflowers turn to follow the Sun across the sky while they are growing.

---

A mushroom is not a plant. It grows in damp, dark places and needs no light at all.

---

Worms are useful in a garden because their tunnels let air and water reach plant
roots.
