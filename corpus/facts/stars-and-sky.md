---

Stars are enormous balls of hot glowing gas, far bigger than Earth, but they look
tiny because they are so far away.

---

Stars cannot be reached or carried. Even the nearest one after the Sun would take
longer than a lifetime to travel to.

---

The Sun is a star, and it is the closest one to Earth.

---

Stars seem to twinkle because their light wobbles as it passes through the moving
air above us. Seen from space, they do not twinkle at all.

---

A shooting star is not a star at all. It is a small piece of rock or dust burning
up as it falls into the air above Earth.

---

Stars are still in the sky during the day. Sunlight is simply so bright that we
cannot see them.

---

The stars appear to move slowly across the night sky because Earth is turning, not
because the stars are travelling.
