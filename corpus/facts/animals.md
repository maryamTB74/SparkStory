Penguins cannot fly. They use their wings as flippers and swim very fast instead.

---

An owl can turn its head much further than it can move its eyes, which is why it
seems to look right over its own shoulder.

---

A snail carries its shell on its back and cannot leave it, because the shell grows
as part of the snail.

---

Bats are not birds. They have fur, and they find their way in the dark by making
sounds and listening for the echoes.

---

A bee visits many flowers and carries pollen between them, which is how the flowers
make seeds.

---

A frog begins life as a tadpole with a tail and no legs, and grows legs as it gets
older.

---

A cat's whiskers help it judge whether a gap is wide enough to squeeze through.

---

Rabbits' eyes are on the sides of their heads, so they can see almost all the way
around without turning.
