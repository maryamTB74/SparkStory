---

Rain begins as tiny drops of water in clouds, which join together until they are
heavy enough to fall.

---

Wind is moving air. It cannot be seen, only felt, and watched in the things it
pushes.

---

A rainbow appears when sunlight shines through falling rain, and it is always
opposite the Sun.

---

Snow is made of ice crystals, and each one has six sides.

---

Thunder is the sound made by lightning heating the air very suddenly. The light
arrives before the sound, which is why the flash comes first.

---

Clouds are made of countless tiny water droplets floating in the air, which is why
a cloud cannot be stood on or held.

---

Puddles disappear on a warm day because the water turns into vapour and rises into
the air, too small to see.
