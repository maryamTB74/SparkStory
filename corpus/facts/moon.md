---
title: The Moon
source: NASA -- Earth's Moon
licence: public domain
---

The Moon has no air around it, so there is no wind there and nothing can flutter or
drift.

---

Because the Moon has no air, sound cannot travel across it. Two people standing
side by side could not hear each other shout.

---

The Moon's gravity is much weaker than Earth's, so a person there could jump far
higher and would come down slowly.

---

The same side of the Moon always faces Earth, so from Earth we never see its far
side.

---

The Moon has no light of its own. It looks bright because sunlight bounces off it.

---

The Moon's surface is covered in fine grey dust and dents called craters, made by
rocks crashing into it long ago.

---

Footprints left in Moon dust stay for a very long time, because there is no wind or
rain to wipe them away.

---

The Moon takes about a month to travel all the way around Earth, which is why it
seems to change shape a little each night.

---

There is no water to swim in on the Moon, but there is ice hidden in deep craters
where sunlight never reaches.

---

The sky above the Moon is always black, even in the middle of the day, because
there is no air to scatter the sunlight into blue.
