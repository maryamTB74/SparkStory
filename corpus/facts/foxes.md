Foxes are most active at dusk and at night, and they sleep through much of the day.

---

A fox's hearing is sharp enough to find a mouse moving underneath snow.

---

Foxes cannot climb well and cannot fly, but they can leap and can squeeze through
narrow gaps.

---

A fox family lives in a burrow called a den, usually dug into a bank or under tree
roots.

---

A fox's tail is long and thick, and it wraps around itself like a blanket in cold
weather.

---

Foxes do not bark like dogs. They make a high, thin call that carries a long way at
night.

---

Young foxes are called cubs. They stay near the den until they are big enough to
hunt.
